# Limitations and responsible interpretation

1. The dataset represents eight selected countries rather than the entire world.
2. Detailed cross-tabulations are published estimates derived from sampled
   records. Small cells can contain sampling error.
3. A value represents passenger movements, not unique visitors.
4. A zero in a detailed cell does not necessarily prove that no person arrived.
5. The August 2016 processing and imputation change can affect long-term
   comparisons.
6. Border restrictions from 2020 and the later reopening created a structural
   break. The final tree comparison excluded March 2020 through February 2022,
   while the descriptive recovery story retained the disruption as context.
7. The dataset does not include airfare, capacity, exchange rates, visa policy
   or economic conditions, so it cannot explain the causes of market changes.
8. Variable importance describes the fitted random forest and does not establish
   causal influence.
9. Results apply to the selected response, predictors and test period.
