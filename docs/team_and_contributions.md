# Team and contribution statement

This project originated as the **Remote Data Miners** group project for STAT462
Data Mining at the University of Canterbury.

Sahil Mann contributed to the research framing, integration of the reproducible
R workflow, checking and reconciling model results, development of comparison
visuals, and coordination of the final presentation and poster narrative.

The repository describes the work as a group project. It intentionally omits
private peer-review comments and does not rank individual contributions. Other
members' names and profile links can be added after public-sharing permission
and exact spelling are confirmed.
